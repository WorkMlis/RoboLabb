How every robot works

Every robot does three things,
again and again.

A robot vacuum, a Mars rover and the car you'll build in class all run the same loop. Learn this and you understand robots.

Step one

Sense

Sensors are the robot's eyes and ears. Sonar measures distance, infrared spots lines on the floor, buttons feel a press.

Step two

Think

The Arduino runs your code over and over, very fast. Your if and else decide what happens next.

Step three

Act

Motors, servos, LEDs, buzzers and screens turn decisions into movement, light and sound. Then the loop starts again.

sense → think → act → repeat, many times every second
